How we manage a booking

A good enquiry starts well before anyone hits Send.

We can connect the website you already use or give your partners a private catalogue. In the catalogue, you decide which properties appear first. Partners can filter by date and by what their client is looking for. When they find the right property, they can view the gallery, check the calendar or download a brochure to send to their client.

When they send the enquiry, it goes straight into your PMS with the property, dates and details you need to act on it. Your team saves the work of copying it all over. In the dashboard, you can see who’s handling the enquiry, where it stands and what’s still outstanding. We set up those steps around the way your business works.

It’s built around you, so you stay in control. So well tailored to you that you won’t even have to use it.

You can open it whenever you want to see how things are going or when there’s a decision to make. We take care of the rest.

You’ve seen how an enquiry comes in. Now we’ll show you what happens when you decide to go ahead. You set the terms and choose who can approve the booking.

Then you talk to the assistant on WhatsApp. You can send a message, a voice note, a photo or a document. You can ask it to get the missing details, get the contract ready for review or follow up on a payment.

Before we put it to work, we sit down with your team to see how the business runs day to day. That’s how we decide which steps the assistant can take and what permissions it has in your PMS. Every task is logged in the dashboard, so you can see what’s been done and what’s still pending.

Confirming the booking is just the beginning. There are still documents to request, contracts to sign and payments to follow up on.

The assistant can ask the client for any missing details, prepare the contract for you to review and send a reminder if it’s still waiting to be signed. Once signed, the contract is saved in the booking record. It’ll always be there if you need it later.

That same record holds the client’s or company’s details, the agreed terms and the transaction history. You can see how much has been paid, what’s outstanding and whether any proof of payment is missing.

You can step in at any point. We set up the follow-up with you, based on how you already work. You have your own platform and access to your data, and you decide which steps run automatically and which come to you.

And of course, some of the work happens at the property.

It needs to be ready before the guest arrives. Someone needs to welcome them, coordinate their departure and know who to call if something breaks during the stay.

That’s a lot of work...

How we build our software and protect your data

We store code and data separately. We keep a version history for the application code and test changes in a separate environment before release. If an update causes a problem, we can roll back to an earlier version of the software. Bookings, payments and documents are managed separately, so rolling back the code leaves your business records in place.

We check permissions where the data lives. The server verifies who is making each request and what they can view or change. The database behind the private catalogue also enforces access rules for individual records. Contracts and payment receipts are kept in private storage. For example, owners can access only the authorised information for their properties.

We plan backups around recovery. We’re putting in place a plan for regular, encrypted backups of code, data and private files. The plan includes checking each part separately and testing restores in an isolated environment so we know what can be recovered and from which point in time.

The agent works with defined tools and permissions. We’re integrating the OpenAI API on the server. The model interprets the request and proposes an action. The application is designed to check permissions before carrying it out. Drafting a contract and authorising its delivery can require different permissions. You decide when approval is needed.

Frequently asked questions

What can I hire you for? Can I get just the software or automation?

Managed booking operations are our main service. They combine software, automation and the day-to-day work you agree to hand over. If your team runs operations, you can commission tailored software, automation or a WhatsApp assistant setup as a stand-alone project.

What does the full booking management service include?

We can handle enquiries from guests and agencies, follow up on bookings, request documents and signatures, and check payments and owner payouts. We agree which tasks we’ll take on and who handles each one. Your dashboard always shows what’s done, what’s outstanding and what needs your approval.

How do you notify my team about arrivals and departures?

We use the check-in and check-out dates in each property’s calendar to schedule email or WhatsApp notifications. Using the contacts saved in the system, we agree who gets each message and when. The person handling check-in, the cleaning or maintenance team, and the property manager each receive the information they need for their task.

To set this up, you’ll need a team already in place and someone assigned to each service.

Who confirms bookings and approves exceptions?

You choose who can confirm a booking or authorise a discount or refund. If we set up an assistant, we define what it can access and which tasks it can carry out. If availability needs checking or a request falls outside the agreed terms, it goes to the person you appoint.

Can I keep my current software and booking channels?

We start by reviewing your existing software to see what can be connected. For Airbnb, a compatible channel manager can sync rates and availability. If the connection uses iCal alone, it syncs booked dates and calendar blocks. Rates are managed separately.

Can we get started without disrupting the busy season?

We can begin with a few bookings or a single task, such as following up on proof of payment. We test it with your team and agree when to roll it out further. Changes with a greater impact on day-to-day work can wait until things are quieter.

How much does it cost, and what’s included in the quote?

It depends on what you want to delegate, which tools need connecting and what the setup involves. The proposal covers what’s included in setup, the cost of any ongoing service you choose and any third-party licences you’ll need.

How will I know if it’s saving my team time?

We agree what to measure before we start. That might be how long it takes to reply to an enquiry or how many payment receipts are outstanding each week. We compare that measure before and after to see whether to adjust or expand the service.

Who can see my bookings, documents and payments?

We agree what each person needs to see and check access before granting it. An owner can see the information approved for their properties. A partner sees the property details and rates you choose to share with them.

What happens to support and my data if I leave?

We put in writing who handles support, what access your team will have and what information it can export. That way, you know what you can take with you if you leave and what depends on the tools that are connected.

What if another booking comes in tomorrow?

Someone has to check the dates. Someone has to ask for proof of payment. And if something goes wrong, someone ends up asking you.
