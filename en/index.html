Airbnb took a step out of booking.

Booking a home on Airbnb used to mean sending a request and waiting for the host. With Instant Book, guests could pick their dates and book straight away on listings that had it switched on.

In 2017, Airbnb reported that six in ten bookings were already made that way. That same year, it announced that hosts would be able to link their calendars to prevent double bookings. The missing message was the bit guests could see. The availability still had to line up behind the scenes.

How TRICOT began.

More than 100 property transactions handled

I’m Tomas A. Pessino, the founder of TRICOT. I started at Housefy, working on property sales, lettings and mortgages. While I was there, I qualified as a registered property agent in Catalonia. I went on to work in consulting, designing tools alongside software developers.

I built the first TRICOT tool at EB Capital Consulting when it started managing rentals. We needed to share properties with partners and see what was happening with each enquiry. When others in the network saw it, they wanted to use it too.

I still start the same way. I sit down with your team and see how you work. Sometimes it’s enough to connect what you already use. Sometimes it makes sense to build something around the way you work.

Delegate tasks, not control.

If your team runs the operation, TRICOT adapts the tools and sets up the agreed automations. Your team still manages bookings, checks paperwork and handles issues.

If you delegate some of the work, we take care of the processes you choose. We agree what we can handle and what has to go to you or someone you appoint. An exceptional discount or refund needs their approval.

Either way, you always have access to your dashboard.

You can see your bookings, outstanding paperwork and payments, what’s been done and who’s responsible for the next step.

Before you ask.

Do I have to switch software?

We start by looking at what you already use. If it supports the connection you need, we can work with it.

Can I start with one task?

Yes. We can start somewhere specific, like requesting proof of payment and seeing which bookings are still waiting for it.

Who can confirm a booking?

You decide. The agent checks and follows up within the rules we agree. Bookings that need a review go to the person you appoint.

Where would we start?

Tell me what happens from the moment an enquiry comes in to the moment you confirm a booking. We can use that to find the first task worth automating or delegating.
