If you delegate some of the work, we take care of the processes you choose. We agree what we can handle and what has to go to you or someone you appoint. An exceptional discount or refund needs their approval.

Before you ask.

Can I keep my current software? What about Airbnb?

We can work with your existing tools if they support the connection you need. We review where you manage bookings, availability and payments. If an integration has limits, we explain what you can keep and what would need to change before you decide.

For Airbnb, we check what your setup supports. A compatible channel manager can send rates and availability to Airbnb. iCal can sync booked dates and calendar blocks. Rates are managed separately.

Can I start with one task?

Yes. For example, we can start by requesting proof of payment and tracking which bookings are still waiting for it. We agree how it should work, test it with your team and review the results before deciding whether to expand.

Can we roll it out without disrupting our busy season?

We can start with one process and test it on a few bookings before rolling it out more widely. We agree what your team needs to check and when to switch it on. If a busy period is coming up, we can schedule more sensitive changes for later.

What if the calendar, WhatsApp and the spreadsheet disagree?

We agree where your team should check each piece of information, such as availability or payments. If the sources disagree, the case goes for review before anyone uses that information to reply to a customer or confirm a booking.

Who can confirm a booking? What if the WhatsApp agent is unsure?

You decide when the agent can act and when it should hand the conversation to a person. It can answer questions and follow up within the rules we agree. If a special rate or availability needs checking, it passes the case to whoever you appoint.

Who can see bookings, customer details and payments?

We agree what each role needs to see and check access before granting it. For example, an owner can view their own bookings and payouts, while a partner can see the property details and rates you choose to share.

What can I hand over, and which decisions stay with me?

You can delegate booking follow-ups, document and payment checks, or updates to your property inventory. We agree who handles each case and who approves exceptions such as a discount or refund. You can still see the status of every booking in your dashboard.

How much does it cost, and what’s included in the quote?

It depends on the process, the tools we need to connect and whether you want to delegate part of the work. The proposal sets out what setup includes, any potential third-party costs, and the cost of support or ongoing operations if you choose them.

How will I know if it’s saving my team time?

We choose a measure that matters to your team, such as how long it takes to answer an enquiry or how many bookings are still waiting for proof of payment. We compare it before and after the first phase to see whether to expand or adjust.

Who looks after it afterwards? What if I switch providers?

Up front, we agree who handles support, what access your team will have and what information it can export. If you decide to switch providers, those agreements show what you can take with you and what depends on the connected tools.

What if another booking comes in tomorrow?

Someone has to check the dates. Someone has to ask for proof of payment. And if something goes wrong, someone ends up asking you.
