What TRICOT is

The business grew. The operation did not have time to catch up.

TRICOT is a software, automation and AI consultancy for businesses with complex or fragmented operations. We first understand how the work runs, which information can be trusted and where human judgment is required. We then connect existing tools or build a tailored system, implement it with the team and define how its usefulness will be measured.

Information lives across messages, spreadsheets, folders and separate tools.

Statuses change depending on who checks. Reporting is rebuilt. Exceptions rely on memory.

Automating that disorder does not fix it. It accelerates it.

TRICOT therefore begins with sources, owners, states and decisions. Only then do we define what should be kept, configured, connected or built.

Which information is authoritative?Who owns each state?Which exception needs a person?How will we know the change helps?
