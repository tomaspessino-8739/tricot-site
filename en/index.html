Services

From setup to day-to-day operations.

  1. 01

    Tailored software

    We start with our booking, catalogue and owner portal tools and adapt them to your business. If you already have a system that works for you, we can integrate it.

  2. 02

    Workflow automation

    We look at where your team repeats work and automate the tasks we agree on: requesting documents, sending reminders or flagging outstanding items.

  3. 03

    WhatsApp agent

    We design an AI agent to answer guest enquiries and help your team check and follow up on bookings from WhatsApp. You decide what it can do and when a person steps in.

  4. 04

    Managed back-office operations

    If you choose to delegate, we handle the agreed processes: following up bookings through confirmation, checking documents, signatures and proof of payment, and keeping your property inventory up to date.

Case studies

From business need to work delivered.

Implementation · Sales and rentals

EB Capital Consulting

When the firm added a real estate practice, we implemented the partner catalogue and its property and deal management tools: 01 / Share and 02 / Manage. Partners can find property details in one place and the team can track each enquiry without piecing together its history.

What to measure: monthly hours spent preparing listings and following up, time to first response, conversion from enquiries to closed deals, and outstanding commissions.

Functional design · Management software

Lone Wolf LLC

We brought property management systems expertise to the design of features around clients’ daily work: what information each task needs, who handles it and how it moves to the next step.

What to measure: minutes and steps per task, usage by feature, issues per hundred operations, and monthly hours spent on support or rework.

Consulting · Hotel distribution

Aguamadera Ibiza

We advised the hotel on connecting its property management system (PMS) with a channel manager to synchronise rates, availability and bookings with platforms such as Booking.com. We defined the setup for the hotel team to implement; we did not carry out the integration.

What to measure after rollout: time spent updating channels, availability discrepancies and duplicate bookings.

Full implementation · Managed operations

Infinity 8 Real Estate

We implemented the catalogue, property and booking management tools, and owner portal, and took on agreed operational processes. The team reduced its administrative workload, improved deal closures and used property, booking and client data to secure new exclusive listings.

What to track: admin hours per booking, close rate, new exclusive listings and profitability by property.

AI agents · Analysis and administration

OPAL Investments

We built agents with Codex to compare investment proposals against consistent criteria, prepare accounting reviews and follow up on administrative tasks. From a phone, the team can resume a task, review the outcome and handle an approval without returning to the desk. The work runs on the connected computer.

What to measure: time per proposal, accounting preparation hours, outstanding documents and time to respond to issues away from the desk.

    The person responsible checks the proof when it arrives and decides whether to confirm.
