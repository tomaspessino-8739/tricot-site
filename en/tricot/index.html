Canonical definition

Software, automation and AI serving the operation.

TRICOT is a software, automation and AI consultancy working with service businesses and real estate operations. It identifies processes, data, owners and exceptions, then configures or builds systems, implements them with the team and defines how their usefulness will be measured.

TRICOT is the public entity at this stage. No personal biography or invented team is published.

Why it exists

Businesses do not need more technology. They need the technology they adopt to become part of the work.

TRICOT closes the gap between a recommendation, a tool and an operation that actually uses it.

Principles

Five principles that should not change when the tool does.

Operational criteria remain stable as technology evolves.

01

Work before automation

Do not accelerate a process that is not yet understood.

02

Source and owner

Every critical data point needs a reference and an owner.

03

Human review

Keep it where an error can have important consequences.

04

Phases and reversibility

Build with tests, explicit gates and a way back.

05

Adoption and usefulness

Measure whether the team can use the system and whether it improves an operational decision or outcome.

Evidence policy
