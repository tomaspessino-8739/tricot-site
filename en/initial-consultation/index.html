Direct answer

A conversation to decide the next step. Not a free audit.

TRICOT's free initial consultation is a conversation to understand an operational problem and determine whether there is a fit. We review the information before offering a booking link. It may lead to a direct proposal, a paid assessment, a specific follow-up or an honest no-fit decision. It does not include a full audit or detailed solution design.

No duration or response time is published until real availability and a sustainable SLA exist.

When it makes sense

You do not need to have the solution. You do need a real problem.

We look for a recognisable situation, a consequence and people who can explain how the work runs today.

01

Scattered information

The team reconstructs decisions across messages, spreadsheets and tools.

02

Repeated work

People move data or repeat tasks that follow stable logic.

03

Personal dependency

An important part of the operation lives in someone's memory.

04

Insufficient control

The current system does not cover important states, permissions or exceptions.

FAQ

Before submitting the context.

The page explains the process even though lead capture is not active yet.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. It is a free initial conversation reviewed in advance. A deeper assessment, when needed, is proposed separately.

Why do you review before sharing Calendar?

To determine whether there is an operational problem, whether TRICOT can help and whether the right people will join.

What information do you need?

Contact identity, business, role, type of operation, problem, consequence and reason to review it now.

How long does it take?

The duration will be published when real and stable availability exists.

Can I use Gmail or apply without a website?

Yes. A free email or no website reduces available traceability but never creates an automatic rejection.
