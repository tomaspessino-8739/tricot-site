02 / Manage

Property and
booking management

Receive enquiries from the catalogue, your website and the channels we connect. Your team can assess each enquiry, collect guest details and confirm the booking.

  • Properties, contacts and documents in one place.
  • An occupancy calendar with arrivals and departures.
  • Booking amounts and the shares due to your business, partners and property owners.
  • Incoming payments, outgoing payments and financial records ready to export for accounting.

Every booking under control, from start to finish.
