What you can do

Everyone has
what they need.

01 / Share

Partner
catalogue

Your network of partners can find properties, availability, prices, photos, videos and documents in one application.

They can offer properties to their clients and send you enquiries without asking for every detail separately.

Fewer repeated questions. An easier way for partners to offer your properties.

02 / Manage

Property and
booking management

Receive enquiries from the catalogue, your website and the channels we connect. Your team can assess each enquiry, collect guest details and confirm the booking.

  • Properties, contacts and documents in one place.
  • An occupancy calendar with arrivals and departures.
  • Booking amounts and the shares due to your business, partners and property owners.
  • Incoming payments, outgoing payments and financial records ready to export for accounting.

Each booking and its finances, managed in the same place.

03 / Inform

Property owner
portal

Each property owner has private access to their bookings and the financial information relevant to them.

They can see how much has been collected, their share, payments received and amounts still outstanding.

Clear, accessible accounts, without having to ask for a summary each time.
